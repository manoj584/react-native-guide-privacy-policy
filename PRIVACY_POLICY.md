# Privacy Policy for React Native Guide

**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

React Native Guide is an educational application designed to help users learn React Native development. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from our users. The app is designed to work entirely offline and does not require any user registration or account creation.

## 3. Data Storage

No user data is stored on our servers. All app data is stored locally on your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services for analytics purposes, which may collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the app.

## 5. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:
- Internet access: For loading online content and resources
- Vibration: For haptic feedback (optional)

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
